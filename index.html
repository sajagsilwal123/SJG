Inspire to Aspire.

Over the last few years, I've worn more hats than I can count—software engineer, co-founder & CEO, educator, project supervisor, backend developer, and occasionally, the guy fixing the printer.

I've mentored over 900 students, co-founded a technology company, architected software that transforms paperwork into intelligent systems, worked with startups and established businesses, and still find myself obsessing over clean APIs, scalable architectures, thoughtful user experiences, and documentation that people actually enjoy reading.

Beyond technology, I'm deeply fascinated by the capital markets and the intersection of finance, business, and technology. With more than seven years of exposure to Nepal's stock market, I've developed analytical tools, explored quantitative approaches to investing, and continue to study how technology can create better financial systems. To deepen this perspective, I am currently pursuing a Master's in Finance at Peking University HSBC Business School (PHBS) in Shenzhen, China, where I'm expanding my understanding of global finance, investment, and strategic decision-making.

"Technology is simply one way I try to solve meaningful problems."

Whether I'm designing scalable backend systems, mentoring future engineers, modernizing the operations of a 30-year-old transport company, or exploring new ideas at the intersection of engineering and finance, I enjoy turning complexity into simple, reliable, and lasting solutions.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me reading, travelling, playing the guitar, exploring new business ideas, analyzing markets, or wondering how Apple would have designed the same product.

My Story

At the intersection of engineering, education, and finance

My career has been defined by a constant crossing of boundaries. As a computer engineer, I am driven by the challenge of architecting systems that scale and endure. As an educator, my focus is on sharing that knowledge and bridging the gap between theory and industry realities. As a co-founder and entrepreneur, I build businesses and products that solve structural inefficiencies, such as modernizing traditional logistics systems at Basuki Transport.

I believe that real engineering isn't just about writing code; it's about solving real-world challenges and designing processes that work efficiently even without constant supervision. This philosophy guides how I build tech, analyze markets, and mentor students.
